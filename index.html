ABOUT BASIS NEXUS

A practical SAP BASIS knowledge hub.

BASIS Nexus is a technical learning and interview platform created to explain SAP BASIS in simple, practical language. The focus is not memorising T-codes — it is understanding what to check, why to check it, what evidence to collect, how to fix the issue and how to validate the result.

The site covers SAP administration, Linux, HANA, S/4HANA, transports, performance, security, certificates, migrations, cloud connectivity, HA/DR, production incidents and RCA.
